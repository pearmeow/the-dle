# Tabletop detective artwork

Created with the built-in image generation tool for this redesign.

- `jev.png`: original detective mascot illustration.
- `covers.png`: the original six-illustration concept atlas, preserved as reference; no longer used for game choices.
- `game-covers/`: matching game artwork downloaded from Steam. Source and store URLs are recorded per title in `game-covers.json`. Game artwork belongs to its respective owners. Unsupported titles use a typographic game-file card.
- `paper.svg`: lightweight repeatable paper flecks drawn as native vector artwork.
- `Bitter.ttf` and `Lato-Regular.ttf`: bundled open-license fonts from the Google Fonts repository. License texts are included alongside the fonts.

## Generation prompts

### Jev

Create a game asset illustration: a charming small round cream detective creature named Jev wearing a brown tweed deerstalker cap and coat, black oval eyes, tiny smile, blush cheeks, holding magnifying glass, seated beside a mug and three little books. Hand drawn pencil contours and warm watercolor on ivory, indie tabletop mystery aesthetic. Full character centered, no words or letters, plain warm ivory #f5efdf background, portrait composition, no interface. Polished expressive restrained illustration.

### Cover atlas

A game asset texture atlas, landscape ratio 3:2. EXACTLY six equal rectangular illustration panels arranged in a strict 3 column by 2 row grid edge-to-edge, no gutters, no text, no letters, no borders. Each panel is a beautifully illustrated watercolor and ink vintage video game adventure book cover, same restrained warm paper art direction. Top left: gothic horror manor corridor with crimson door. Top middle: vintage fighter airplane over mountains blue sky. Top right: fantasy adventurers with castle and dragon in ochre and olive forest. Bottom left: retro science fiction astronaut exploring alien desert with ringed planet, teal and ochre. Bottom middle: cozy colorful village and friendly playful creatures, coral and green. Bottom right: abstract geometric puzzle blocks in warm coral mustard teal with paper texture. Rich detailed handmade illustration with clean silhouettes, no UI, no titles.

## Title-screen background

`title-background.png` was made with the built-in image generation tool from the approved title-screen concept. Text and buttons are rendered by Godot, not baked into the image.

Prompt: Edit this approved game title screen to produce ONLY its background illustration asset. Remove ALL text, letters, numbers, title, date, captions, words on books, buttons, button borders, underline strokes, UI and menus. Preserve the charming detective creature Jev on the RIGHT, the cozy study, golden window light, tea, magnifying glass, bookshelves and desk. Keep the LEFT 48 percent as quiet light cream parchment with very restrained texture and no foreground objects or strong dark shadows; this space will hold real rendered game UI text and buttons. Preserve the layout, warm watercolor and pencil illustration, beautiful lighting, landscape 16:10 composition. No text anywhere. Not a UI screenshot: illustration background only.
